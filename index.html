Zoomies

Motion games for the whole house: play by moving in front of your iPhone's camera, on the phone or on your TV.

Support

Something not working, or a question? Email zoomiesgame@gmail.com and say which iPhone you have and what happened.

Tips

  • Stand where the camera can see you from head to knees, about two to three metres away, with the light in front of you rather than behind.
  • To play on a TV, connect the phone with AirPlay or a cable. Settings > Screen delay measures the TV's lag so the games stay in time with you.
  • Zoomies Plus is managed in your App Store account: Settings > your name > Subscriptions.

Privacy

Zoomies collects no personal information. The camera is used on the phone only, never recorded or sent. Read the privacy policy.
