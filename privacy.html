Zoomies Privacy Policy

Last updated 11 October 2026

The short version: Zoomies does not collect, store or share any personal information. Your camera picture is used on your device, in the moment, to see how you move, and is never recorded or sent anywhere. There are no accounts, no ads, no tracking and no analytics.

What Zoomies is

Zoomies is a motion game for iPhone. It uses the camera to see where your body and hands are so you can play by moving, on the phone's screen or on a TV connected to it.

The camera

Zoomies asks to use the camera because the games are played by moving in front of it. Each picture is used on the phone itself to find the position of your body and hands, then discarded. Pictures are never saved, never sent to us or to anyone else, and no face recognition or identification takes place. The tracking is done by software running entirely on your phone (Apple Vision and Google's MediaPipe), which does not send anything off the device.

What stays on your phone

  • Player names, scores, trophies and settings you choose are kept in the app's own folder on your phone. They are not sent to us. Deleting the app deletes them.
  • If you turn on Record in Settings, the app saves the tracked positions of the body and hands, not video, to the app's folder for tuning the tracking. It never leaves the phone.

Playing with a friend

Play with a friend connects two phones directly over the same Wi-Fi network using a room code, or through Apple's Game Center. Only the game's moves travel between the two phones. Game Center is run by Apple under Apple's privacy policy; we receive nothing from it.

Zoomies Plus

Zoomies Plus is a subscription bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment and your payment details; we never see them. The app only learns whether your subscription is active. Subscriptions are managed and cancelled in your App Store account settings.

Children

Zoomies is made for families, and the same rules apply to everyone who plays: we do not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. The app shows no advertising, has no accounts, no chat and no links out to other services beyond the App Store's own purchase and subscription screens.
